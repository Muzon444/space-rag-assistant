Space exploration has helped scientists understand planets, stars, galaxies, and the universe more deeply.

The solar system contains eight planets that orbit around the Sun. Earth is the only known planet that supports life.

Mars is called the Red Planet because its surface contains iron oxide, which gives it a reddish color.

Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system and is known for its Great Red Spot, which is a massive storm.

Black holes are regions in space with extremely strong gravity where even light cannot escape.

The Moon affects ocean tides on Earth because of its gravitational pull.

NASA and other space agencies use satellites and telescopes to study space and collect scientific data.

Astronauts experience microgravity while living and working aboard spacecraft and space stations.

The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our solar system and billions of other stars.

Space telescopes such as the Hubble Telescope allow scientists to observe distant galaxies and stars more clearly.

Rockets are used to transport satellites, astronauts, and scientific equipment into space.

Scientists continue to search for signs of life on other planets and moons in the universe.
